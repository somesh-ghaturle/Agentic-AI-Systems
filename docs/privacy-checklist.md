# Privacy & Data Protection Checklist

Key privacy controls for AI systems that process personal or sensitive data.

1. Legal Basis & DPIA
   - Document the legal basis for processing (consent, contract, legitimate interest).
   - Perform Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk systems.

2. Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation
   - Collect only required fields and retain data for minimal necessary periods.

3. PII Handling
   - Classify data by sensitivity and apply stronger controls for high-risk data.
   - Anonymize or pseudonymize data where feasible.

4. Consent & Subject Rights
   - Implement mechanisms for data subject requests (access, deletion, portability).

5. Logging & Auditing
   - Log data access and processing actions; protect logs from tampering.

6. Cross-border Data Transfer
   - Ensure legal mechanisms (SCCs, adequacy) for cross-border transfers.

7. Secure Storage & Transmission
   - Encrypt data at rest and in transit; use managed key services where possible.

8. Third-party Processors
   - Contractually ensure processors meet privacy and security obligations.

9. Retention & Deletion
   - Automate data retention policies and secure deletion workflows.

10. Training & Awareness
   - Train engineers and reviewers on privacy-aware data handling.

Adapt this checklist to local regulations and sector-specific privacy rules.
